About

I’m a full-stack engineer from Lagos. For the last four years I’ve built products from end to end: the database, the backend, the web and mobile apps on top, and the servers they run on.

That means one person understands your whole product. No passing work between five freelancers, and no finger-pointing when something breaks. I’ve done it for an online store, a marketplace, a news site and a handful of apps, and lately I’ve been doing a lot with AI, including building my own assistant, FREYA.

I’m taking on freelance projects now, and I also do technical consultations if you want a second opinion before you build. If you’ve got a store to launch, an agent to build, a dashboard your team will actually use, or an old system that needs rescuing, I’d love to hear about it.
