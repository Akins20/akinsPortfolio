Play Store review

A new maze every time

Every level is generated on the spot, so no two games are the same. Before you ever see a maze, the game floods it from the start to prove every corner can be reached. No trapped pellets and no levels you can’t finish. The maze at the top of this page is doing exactly that, live in your browser.

Enemies that keep you guessing

There are four kinds of enemy, each with its own way of hunting you. They switch between spreading out and closing in, in waves, so the pressure builds, eases off, then builds again.

Co-op without lag

Two players can team up online through a small Go server. One phone runs the game while the other predicts its own movement and quietly corrects itself, so neither player feels any delay. If someone’s connection drops, they have ninety seconds to come back and take their seat.
